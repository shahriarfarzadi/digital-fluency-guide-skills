# Digital Thinking Playbook

## Core concepts

### Unlearning mental models
Industrial-era assumptions often optimize for predictability, ownership, scale through repetition, and top-down control. Digital environments reward learning speed, recombination, participation, and network coordination. Do not label an assumption obsolete without naming the changed condition that invalidates it.

Use this table:

| Assumption | Evidence it helped before | Changed condition | New possibility | Small test |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Incremental versus exponential
Incremental work improves an existing system. Exponential work changes the system’s growth mechanism through networks, software, data, automation, or participation. Protect the two modes from incompatible approval rules and metrics.

For a 10x hypothesis, specify the mechanism: more participants, more reuse, more data feedback, lower marginal cost, or a new relationship between resources. A large ambition without a mechanism is not an exponential strategy.

### Shared purpose
Shared purpose is a practical coordination device. It should explain whom the work serves, what change it seeks, why the organization is suited to contribute, and how others can participate. It is not a slogan or substitute for ownership.

### Thinking styles and thought partnership
Invite distinct ways of framing, testing, operating, and imagining. Ask each partner what they notice, what they doubt, and what evidence would change their view. Capture the decision impact of the conversation.

### Remote collaboration
Design a workplan around outcomes, decision rights, documentation, channels, response expectations, meeting purpose, inclusion, and review cadence. Presence is not a proxy for contribution.

## Diagnostic prompts

- Which current rule assumes scarcity, location, hierarchy, or one-way value?
- What would have to be true for a 10x result?
- Which stakeholder must co-create for the model to work?
- Which thinking style or lived experience is missing?
- Which remote-work agreement is implicit and therefore unreliable?

## Experiment patterns

1. Replace one approval step with a principle and bounded decision rights.
2. Run a two-week thought-partnership review on a live strategic question.
3. Rewrite one process as an outcome-based remote workplan.
4. Test a shared-purpose statement with internal and external participants.

## Failure modes

- Calling every ambitious target exponential.
- Using “culture” to avoid defining a decision or owner.
- Treating disagreement as resistance.
- Moving a co-located process online without redesigning communication.
